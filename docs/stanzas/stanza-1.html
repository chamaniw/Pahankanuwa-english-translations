Pāli Stanza

Source: Dhammapada 1 | Referenced in: Sermon 01

Pāli Text

Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā
manoseṭṭhā manomayā

English Translation

Mind precedes all mental states; mind is their chief, they are mind-made.
